uses independent executable probes. Review, receipt and recovery cases have

## Final working idea

Keep one capable owner for dependent work. Use direct scripts for exact record
and source-line extraction. A helper should replace an identified independent
owner task and return evidence the owner can check cheaply. Use independent
review at consequential gates, then reuse its disposition for unchanged inputs.
Reuse passing check receipts only when command, source identity and terminal
evidence still match. Preserve authority, pause state and the next checkpoint
in the rolling handoff.

This is a provisional operating choice consistent with existing guidance and
the measured failures. No live skill policy or native profile changed. A claim
that delegation improves accepted quality or total cost still needs the
unrun behavioral diagnostics and representative matched held-out tasks.
